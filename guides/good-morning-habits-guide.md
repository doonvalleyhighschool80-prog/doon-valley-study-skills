# Parent's Checklist: Building Good Morning Habits in Your Child

The way a child starts their morning sets the tone for their entire day. A calm, structured morning builds confidence, focus, and a positive learning mindset. Use this checklist to establish a healthy morning routine for your child.

---

## 📋 The Good Morning Habits Checklist

### 1. Sleep & Waking Foundation
- [ ] **Adequate Rest:** The bedtime is early enough to ensure the child gets full, age-appropriate sleep (e.g., 9–11 hours for primary kids).
- [ ] **Consistent Wake-Up Time:** The child wakes up at the same time daily, even on weekends, to stabilize their biological clock.
- [ ] **Buffer Time:** The alarm is set early enough to include a 15–20 minute calm buffer, avoiding rushed tasks.

### 2. Digital Boundaries
- [ ] **Screen-Free Start:** No smartphones, tablets, or television are used for the first 30–60 minutes of the morning.
- [ ] **Healthy Alternatives:** Morning wake-up is accompanied by light stretching, opening windows for natural light, or quick reading.
- *For screen guidelines, refer to:* [Screen Time Guidelines for Children](https://doonvalleylucknow.com/pages/screen-time-guidelines-for-children-a-complete-guide-for-parents)

### 3. Morning Sequence & Organization
- [ ] **Repeatable Task Sequence:** The child follows a fixed order of activities (e.g., wake up ➔ freshen up ➔ get dressed ➔ eat breakfast).
- [ ] **Visual Morning Chart:** A simple written or image-based checklist of tasks is displayed in their bedroom or bathroom.
- [ ] **Night-Before Preparation:** School bags, uniforms, shoe placement, and lunch boxes are prepared the previous evening to save time. See: [Adjusting to School Time Checklist](file:///D:/Codex/Tips/guides/adjusting-to-school-time-guide.md).

### 4. Nutrition & Communication
- [ ] **Healthy Breakfast:** A balanced, low-sugar breakfast is served to sustain the child's focus and energy at school.
- [ ] **Positive Communication:** Morning directions are given calmly, avoiding shouting or stressful rushing commands.
- [ ] **Emotional Check-in:** A few positive words or encouragement are shared before the child departs for school.
