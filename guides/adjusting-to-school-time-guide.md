# Parent's Checklist: Helping Your Child Adjust to School Time

Adjusting to school schedules (waking up early, getting ready on time, and staying focused all day) is a significant transition for children. Use this checklist to establish a smooth morning routine and ease school-day anxiety.

---

## 📋 The School Timing Transition Checklist

### 1. Sleep Schedule Preparation
- [ ] **Gradual Adjustment:** Bedtime and wake-up times are shifted earlier by 10–15 minutes each day starting a week before school starts.
- [ ] **Bedtime Consistency:** Sleep and wake times remain stable on weekends to maintain the biological clock.
- [ ] **Digital Curfew:** Screens and digital entertainment are turned off at least 45 minutes before sleep to ensure restful rest.

### 2. Planning & Schedule Familiarity
- [ ] **Review Timings:** The school's daily schedule has been reviewed with the child (classes, lunch breaks, dispersal).
- [ ] **Check School Hours:** Official school timing guidelines are confirmed: [School Timings Page](https://doonvalleylucknow.com/pages/school-timings-doon-valley-high-school-lucknow).
- [ ] **Orientation Attendance:** New students have attended school orientation sessions to normalize the classroom layout. See: [Doon Valley Orientation Programme](https://doonvalleylucknow.com/pages/orientation-programme-at-doon-valley-high-school-lucknow-welcome-to-academic-excellence).

### 3. Morning Organization
- [ ] **Night-Before Prep:** Uniforms, water bottles, and school bags are prepared and organized the evening before.
- [ ] **Visual Morning Checklist:** A simple written or icon-based checklist (brushing, dressing, breakfast, bag check) is accessible to the child.
- [ ] **Calm Buffer Time:** Waking up 15 minutes earlier than strictly necessary to prevent morning rushing and stress.

### 4. Emotional Support & Motivation
- [ ] **Open Discussions:** Talk with your child daily about their school feelings (nervousness, excitement) without dismissing their concerns.
- [ ] **Positive Association:** Discuss fun aspects of school, such as friends, clubs, and sports. Explore: [Extra-Curricular Activities](https://doonvalleylucknow.com/pages/extra-curricular-activities-doon-valley-high-school-lucknow).
- [ ] **Patience with Transition:** Expecting an adjustment window of 1 to 2 weeks for the child to settle in fully.
