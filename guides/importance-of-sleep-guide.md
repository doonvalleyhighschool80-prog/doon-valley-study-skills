# Parent's Checklist: The Importance of Sleep for Growing Children

Sleep directly influences physical growth, memory retention, emotional balance, and classroom focus. Deep sleep triggers essential growth hormones, consolidates daily learning, and restores immune health. Use this checklist to establish consistent, healthy sleep habits for your child.

---

## 📋 The Children's Sleep Checklist

### 1. Age-Appropriate Sleep Targets & Consistency
- [ ] **Know Age Recommendations:**
  - *Preschoolers (3–5 years):* 10–13 hours (including naps)
  - *Primary School (6–12 years):* 9–12 hours
  - *Teenagers (13–18 years):* 8–10 hours
- [ ] **Consistent Schedule:** Maintain fixed sleep and wake times daily — even on weekends — to stabilize your child's internal body clock.
- [ ] **Treat Sleep as Non-Negotiable:** Prioritize sleep in your family routine alongside academics and extracurriculars. See: [Balanced Daily Routine Guide](file:///D:/Codex/Tips/guides/creating-a-balanced-daily-routine.md).

### 2. Evening Boundaries & Wind-Down Routine
- [ ] **Screen Curfew:** Turn off phones, tablets, and TVs at least 45–60 minutes before bedtime to prevent blue-light sleep disruption.
- [ ] **Predictable Wind-Down Sequence:** Establish a calming pre-bed routine (e.g., warm bath, brushing teeth, light reading) to signal rest to the body.
- [ ] **Light Dinners & Quiet Activity:** Serve lighter evening meals and avoid high-energy games right before bed. See: [Healthy Eating Habits Guide](file:///D:/Codex/Tips/guides/healthy-eating-habits-guide.md).

### 3. Sleep Environment & Holistic Balance
- [ ] **Restful Environment:** Ensure the bedroom is dark, quiet, cool, and free from bright lights or noisy distractions.
- [ ] **Prevent Late-Night Homework:** Manage afternoon study time so academic work doesn't spill late into the night. See: [Good Morning Habits Guide](file:///D:/Codex/Tips/guides/good-morning-habits-guide.md).
- [ ] **Monitor Mood & Focus:** Watch for signs of sleep deficit such as morning irritability, daytime drowsiness, or difficulty concentrating in class. See: [Supporting Mental Well-Being Guide](file:///D:/Codex/Tips/guides/supporting-mental-wellbeing-guide.md).
