# Parent's Guide: Creating a Balanced Daily Routine for Students

A well-structured daily routine is one of the simplest and most effective tools to help children thrive both inside and outside the classroom. Rather than a rigid schedule, a good routine establishes a healthy daily rhythm.

---

## 📋 The Routine Building Blocks Checklist

Use this checklist to design a balanced routine that integrates academic focus, physical health, and emotional well-being.

### 1. Sleep & Recovery
- [ ] **Consistent Bedtime:** Wake-up and bedtimes are roughly the same every day, even on weekends.
- [ ] **Screen-Free Wind Down:** Devices are powered off at least 30–60 minutes before bedtime to support deep sleep.
- [ ] **Sufficient Hours:** Primary students get 9–11 hours of sleep; secondary students get 8–10 hours.

### 2. Focused Academic Blocks
- [ ] **Short Study Slots:** Study sessions are broken into short, high-focus blocks (e.g., 30–45 minutes) rather than long hours.
- [ ] **Specific Study Space:** Homework is completed in a dedicated study area (see our [Study Space Checklist](file:///D:/Codex/Tips/guides/distraction-free-study-space-checklist.md)).
- [ ] **Independent Focus:** Time is set aside for self-study and independent learning to build exam readiness.

### 3. Physical Activity & Play
- [ ] **Outdoor Time:** A minimum of 30–60 minutes of active play, cycling, or outdoor sports is protected daily.
- [ ] **Screen Breaks:** Physical stretches or short movements are encouraged during long study breaks.

### 4. Healthy Screen Boundaries
- [ ] **Clear Timing:** Specific hours are set for entertainment screen use, separate from educational use.
- [ ] **Device-Free Zones:** Mealtimes and bedrooms are designated device-free zones.
- *For detailed screen guidelines, refer to:* [Screen Time Guidelines for Children](https://doonvalleylucknow.com/pages/screen-time-guidelines-for-children-a-complete-guide-for-parents)

### 5. Family & Unstructured Time
- [ ] **Agenda-Free Space:** Part of the evening is kept free for spontaneous play, reading, or family discussion.
- [ ] **Connection Moments:** Regular family meals or shared evening walks are built into the weekly routine.
