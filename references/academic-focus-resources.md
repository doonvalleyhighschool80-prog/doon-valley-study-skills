# Academic Focus & Productivity References

A quick directory of scientifically-backed productivity techniques and tools compiled by the educational team at **Doon Valley High School, Lucknow** to help children build healthy focus habits.

---

## ⏱️ Recommended Study Techniques

### 1. The Pomodoro Technique (Adapted for Children)
- **Concept:** Break study time into manageable chunks followed by physical breaks.
- **For Nursery – Class II:** 15 minutes of study, 5 minutes of physical play/stretch.
- **For Class III – Class VI:** 25 minutes of study, 5 minutes of quiet rest.
- **For Class VII – Class X:** 40 minutes of study, 10 minutes of rest.
- **Best Practice:** Use a physical kitchen timer rather than a phone screen to track intervals.

### 2. Spaced Repetition Method
- **Concept:** Review topics at expanding intervals (1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 14 days) to transfer knowledge into long-term memory.
- **Implementation:** Create physical flashcards or simple study tables.

### 3. Active Recall
- **Concept:** Close the book and write down or explain what was just read, rather than passively highlighting text.
- **Best Practice:** Encourage your child to "teach" you the topic they just studied.
